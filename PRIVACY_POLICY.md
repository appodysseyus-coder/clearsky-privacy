# ClearSky Weather Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026  
**App:** ClearSky Weather for Android (com.appodyssey.clearsky), including HailX Pro  
**Developer:** App Odyssey  
**Contact:** app.odyssey.us@gmail.com

ClearSky Weather shows forecasts, weather alerts, radar, lightning and storm reports for US places. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

## The short version

- ClearSky Weather has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking code, and App Odyssey runs no server for it.
- Your saved places and settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
- To get weather, your phone asks public weather and map services (such as the National Weather Service) directly for data about a place. That request includes the place's map coordinates.
- We don't sell or share your personal information.

## Information we collect
None. The app doesn't send your location, saved places, settings or usage to App Odyssey, so we have no copy of them.

## Information stored only on your device
The app keeps these in its private storage on your phone:

- **Your saved places:** the name and map coordinates of each place you save, and their order.
- **Your current place:** the coordinates of the place you're viewing (from search, a saved place, or your approximate location if you allowed it), so the app, widgets and notifications can show weather for it.
- **Your settings:** units, notification kinds and quiet hours, radar and lightning options, widget opacity and similar choices.
- **Cached data:** recent forecasts, alerts and storm reports, so screens and widgets load quickly. They're refreshed or replaced over time.

City and ZIP search uses data built into the app, so what you type in search never leaves your phone.

You can delete saved places in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove all of it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's settings and saved places in your own device backup, which Google handles under your Google account settings. We can't see those backups.

## Location
ClearSky Weather uses your phone's location only while you're using the app:

- **"My location":** when you allow it, the app asks Android for your approximate location to show weather where you are. It's used to look up the forecast and alerts for that spot.
- **No background location:** the app doesn't request the "Allow all the time" location permission. Widgets and notifications use your saved places or the last place you viewed in the app.

Location is optional. You can search for a city or ZIP code instead, and you can turn location access off at any time in Android settings.

On many phones, Android gets your location through Google Play services, under Google's own privacy policy.

## Notifications
If you turn on weather alerts, rain and snow heads-ups, weather-ahead notes or HailX Pro hail alerts, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Notifications are created on your phone: about every 30 minutes, the app checks public weather data for your place and decides on the phone whether to notify you. No App Odyssey server is involved. Notifications are not an official warning system; always follow the National Weather Service and local officials.

## Where the app gets its data
Your phone requests data directly from these public services. Each request includes the map coordinates of a place or a map area, along with your phone's IP address and a short app name tag, as any internet request does. These services have their own privacy policies. They're government, university or open-data services, not advertisers:

- National Weather Service and NOAA (api.weather.gov, opengeo.ncep.noaa.gov), for forecasts, observations, alerts, warnings and radar
- NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (ncei.noaa.gov), for radar-detected hail history
- NOAA GOES lightning data, from NOAA's public cloud storage on Amazon Web Services (noaa-goes19.s3.amazonaws.com)
- Iowa State University's Iowa Environmental Mesonet (mesonet.agron.iastate.edu), for radar and model map tiles and NWS storm reports
- OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), for the base map built from OpenStreetMap data

## Purchases
HailX Pro will be sold through Google Play. If you buy it, Google Play handles the payment and we don't receive your payment details; the app only learns whether Pro is active on your Google account. During closed testing, Pro features are unlocked for testers and nothing is sold.

## Data sale and sharing
We don't sell, rent or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Third-party code
The app is built with open-source libraries (Capacitor, MapLibre and Android Jetpack). It includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code. Google Play may collect information when you download or update the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

## Children
ClearSky Weather is intended for people 13 and older. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Security
All data requests use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Data stored on your phone is protected by Android's app storage protections.
